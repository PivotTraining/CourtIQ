Inside CourtIQ

Track the game.
Train with a purpose.

A basketball-development workspace built around the events you record—not promises of automatic scouting or player rankings.

🏀

GAMETIME

Record shots, free throws, rebounds, assists, steals, blocks, turnovers, fouls, and minutes. Review the completed game and its shooting splits.

📊

My IQ

Explore recorded performance trends and development priorities. Insights depend on complete, accurate entries; they are not verified scouting grades.

🎯

Skill Lab

Choose from 162 drills, follow guided practice steps, record your own reps, and compare like-for-like completed workouts.

🗺️

Heat maps

See where your recorded shots come from and how results differ by court zone. Missing entries cannot be treated as a complete game.

📝

Journal

Reflect on confidence, focus, and your basketball experience alongside your recorded development work.

↗

Share cards

Create square or story-format cards from your recorded stats. Review the card before downloading or sharing, especially for younger players.

How it works

A clear next move.
Not just more numbers.

1
Play

Record the game

Open GAMETIME, enter what happened, and finish your session. Do not treat the tracker as an official league box score.

2
Understand

Review the evidence

Look at the completed report, shooting zones, and recorded trends. Smaller or incomplete samples carry clear limits.

3
Train

Practice, then repeat

Pick a skill, follow a drill, and record the work. Return after your next game to review your development.

Device video

Your clips.
On your device.

Record with your device camera, then attach a compatible clip to a saved CourtIQ session. This is device-local storage—not automatic full-game recording or a cloud backup.

✓Attach compatible MP4, WebM, or MOV clips
✓100 MB per clip; 250 MB per account on that device
✓Download a copy and keep your own backup
✓Clearing browser data can remove locally stored clips

What video does not include

No unlimited cloud storage, automatic highlight creation, synchronized full-game recording, or verified video-to-stat analysis is included in this web pilot.

Read the pilot boundaries

Recorded work, not guaranteed outcomes

CourtIQ uses self-recorded basketball data. Recommendations and experimental statistical outlooks can be wrong, and do not guarantee athletic improvement, recruiting outcomes, or professional-player potential.
